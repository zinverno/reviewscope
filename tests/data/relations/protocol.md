# Frozen paired synthetic experiment v1

Each TSV line is a distinct immutable authored review version. Order within split gives ID split-NN and the synthetic source is split. Family IDs are namespaced by split. Expand long1/2/3 to 40 repeats of «Во дворе депо рос клён.», «Рядом с буфетом стояла кадка.», «Ветер трепал паруса.» respectively. Decode literal CRLF and remove evidence brackets, retaining half-open Unicode spans. First/second nonempty annotated aspect takes its corresponding brackets; an ambiguous stage uses one shared span. Use the exact queue and handover payloads in decision-definitions.json (synthetic aspect revisions v1 per split). No inference on client text.

Enumerate all 435 direct pairs separately for each split, each aspect, and independently for text. Gold text is exact under lower/split/join, otherwise near_copy within one explicitly authored copy family, otherwise no_relation. Near-copy describes wording even with a fact change. Aspect gold: C/C same_specific_complaint, C/D opposite_stance, otherwise no_relation, except any ? = unjudged, M or U = uncertain. N is a neutral mention. These authored construction labels are dependent across shared endpoints. No unseen-aspect generalization. Machine inputs exclude endpoint labels, family, ID, scores, ratings and gold spans.

Candidate channels: exact, RapidFuzz ratio >=0.65, char_wb TF-IDF (3,5) and pinned MiniLM full-review cosine, directional top-4 union each with minimum cosine 0.10 / 0.40 respectively. No self edges. Raw scores remain separate. Fixed reference predicts near_copy with ratio >=0.85 and at least 12 matching contiguous codepoints, otherwise no_relation. Aspect baseline: conservative stage and denial/complaint rules. Second classifier: balanced logistic regression C=1, seed 24, max_iter=500, trained separately per definition on labelled train pairs only. Symmetric abs difference and elementwise product of train-fitted char TF-IDF vectors, plus ratio and endpoint rule-state count features. No calibration-based threshold search or refit. Both evaluated on same candidates. Calibration records both; select by macro F1 over two positive classes (tie = rules); seal selection before one holdout and identical repeat. Compare per-channel/union retrieval, conditional and end-to-end TP/FP/FN/precision/recall/F1, macro/micro per axis, supports, unknown, abstentions, nonselected. Zero denominator = null. Per-kind gate >=10 positives and >=10 hard negatives, precision >=.85, end-to-end recall >=.80 and zero opposite/shared swaps or common-topic false complaints. Synthetic only. No reliable semantic filter regardless of result.

Cabinet profile: PostgreSQL bounded synchronous projection limited to 32 accepted versions, 4 KiB UTF-8 per text, at most 496 unordered comparisons / result, 10 pairs per page and diagram. Normalized equality; lexical candidate from shared words, frozen human outcomes independently of wording. No dense inference in the cabinet. Offline 10k producer with combinatorial exact count, <=20k candidates, lexical sort-neighbour blocking, total UTF-8 <=4 MiB, 120 seconds and 2 GiB. No partial publication after a limit. No paid calls.
